# Delete your Healti account

## In the app

1. Open Healti and go to **Profile**.
2. Under **Data**, tap **Delete account** (or **Delete my data** if you never
   signed up).
3. Confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and everything in it:

- your profile
- meals, workouts and check-ins

It happens immediately and can't be undone.

## Without the app

Email **naser.kakadost@hotmail.com** from the address you signed up with, with the subject
"Delete my account". We will delete your account and all of the data above
within 30 days, and confirm by email.

## What is kept

- Backups held by our hosting provider expire within 30 days.
