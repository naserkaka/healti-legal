# Healti Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-06_

Healti ("we", "us") is a nutrition, training and wellness app. This policy
explains what we collect, why, who processes it for us, and how to delete it.

**Controller:** Naser Kakadost, 215 68 Malmö, Sweden.
**Contact:** naser.kakadost@hotmail.com

## The short version

- We collect what you log in the app, so the app can show it back to you and
  calculate your targets.
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
- Health data from Health Connect stays on your device. It is never sent to
  us, never used for advertising and never sold.
- Healti does not use AI services. Nothing you log is sent to an AI provider.
- You can delete your account and all of your data in the app at any time:
  **Profile → Data → Delete account**.

## What we collect

**Account.** When you first open the app, we create an anonymous account
identified by a random ID. If you sign up, we also store your email address,
and your name if you sign in with Google.

**Profile.** This is what you enter during setup: age, sex, height, weight,
activity level and goal. We use it to calculate your daily targets.

**What you log.** Meals (names, calories, macros), workouts (exercises, sets,
weights), and daily check-ins and water.

**Barcodes and food searches.** When you scan a barcode or search for a food,
the barcode or search text is sent straight from your phone to Open Food Facts
to look up the product. It is not linked to your account.

**Health data (optional, with your permission).** If you connect Health
Connect (Android), Healti reads steps, active calories, exercise sessions,
weight, resting heart rate and blood pressure.

- The readings are stored and shown only on your device. They are not sent to
  our server.
- You can revoke access at any time in your phone's settings.

**Technical logs.** Our server logs each request's route, status and timing.
These logs contain your anonymous account ID but not what you logged.

We don't collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or device
identifiers.

## Why we use it (legal bases)

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Running the app: storing and showing your log, calculating targets | Account, profile, logs | Contract |
| Reading Health Connect, on your device only | Health data | Explicit consent, given in the system permission screen |
| Security and abuse prevention | Technical logs | Legitimate interest |

Health data is special-category data under GDPR. We process it only with your
explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time by revoking the permission.

## Who processes it for us

We share data only with the providers that run the service, only for the
purposes above, and under their data processing terms:

| Provider | What for | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Firebase (Google LLC) | Sign-in | Account ID, email, name |
| Railway (Railway Corp.) | Server hosting and database | Everything stored in your account |
| Open Food Facts | Barcode and food search lookups, sent straight from your phone | The barcode or search text only, never your account |
| Apple / Google | App distribution | As described in their own policies |

Our server and database are hosted by Railway in the United States (US West).
Firebase may also process data in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, it is
protected by the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses.

We don't sell or rent your personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.

## Health Connect

Healti's use of information received from Health Connect follows the
[Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134),
including its Limited Use requirements. In particular:

- Health data is used only to show you your own summaries in the app, and it
  stays on your device.
- It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to determine credit
  or insurance eligibility.
- People can read it only when you ask us for help with your own data, for
  security purposes, or to comply with the law.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your
account removes it from our database immediately, and from our providers'
backups within 30 days.

Anonymous accounts that are never signed up for are kept like any other
account. You can delete one from **Profile → Data → Delete my data**.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you can:

- ask for a copy of your data
- correct it
- delete it
- restrict or object to its processing
- ask for a portable copy
- withdraw consent

You can delete your data in the app. For anything else, email
naser.kakadost@hotmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Sweden, that is
Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), www.imy.se.

## Children

Healti has no minimum age. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian
before you use Healti, because in Sweden and the rest of the EU a child under 13
needs their consent for us to process their data. If you are a parent or
guardian and believe your child has used Healti without your consent, contact
us and we will delete the account and its data.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Every request is authenticated with a
Firebase ID token and can only reach the account it belongs to. Access to our
database is limited to the people who run Healti.

## Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will say so in the app before
they take effect.
