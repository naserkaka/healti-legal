# Healti Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email **naser.kakadost@hotmail.com** and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No. Healti works from the first launch. Create an account in **Profile** if you want to back up your log and restore it on another device.

**How are my targets calculated?** From the age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal you enter in setup. Change them any time in **Profile**.

**Where does food data come from?** Barcode and search results come from [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a public food database. You can always enter a meal by hand.

**How do I delete my data?** See [Delete your account](../delete-account/).
